Consulta Reforma

Problema: com a Reforma Tributária, empresa que compra de fornecedor do Simples, MEI ou com CNPJ irregular deixa de gerar crédito de CBS/IBS — e quase ninguém sabe quais fornecedores são esses. O que fizemos: digite o CNPJ e veja na hora o regime do fornecedor, se ele gera crédito e o crédito anual estimado. Consulta em lote, relatório em PDF e alertas de mudança de status. Meu papel: a partir do escopo do sócio, especialista no setor, construí o site, a aplicação, a operação e as vendas.

Problem: under Brazil’s Tax Reform, buying from small-business-regime, micro-entrepreneur or irregular suppliers no longer generates CBS/IBS tax credits — and almost nobody knows which suppliers those are. What we built: type a company ID and instantly see the supplier’s regime, whether it generates credit and the estimated annual credit. Batch lookups, PDF reports and status-change alerts. My role: from my co-founder’s scope as the industry expert, I built the website, the application, operations and sales.

Problem: at an accounting firm, every new batch of client companies needs more people doing the same work for everyone, with internal errors and clients who do not send documents — and no operational KPIs at all. What I am doing: on my own, I am building the process, tooling and AI layer for tax, payroll, compliance and productivity, with Claude and APIs, and putting in the metrics that will measure before and after.
